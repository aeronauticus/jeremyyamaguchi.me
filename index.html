About

I’m a career entrepreneur and have spent the last fifteen years building companies at the intersection of technology and home services. I most recently founded Lawn Love—a YC-backed lawn care marketplace–which I grew to 250 corporate employees and 7,000+ field technicians, with operations in 120 cities across 40 states. In 2021 Lawn Love merged with a competitor to create the largest marketplace in the lawn and garden category.

Prior to that I founded another home services company called Golden Shine. We were ranked #5 on the FAST100 list of fastest growing privately owned companies in 2013 and acquired by a PE firm the same year. Prior to that I started a marketing agency in my early 20s, which is where I cut my teeth on growth marketing and lead gen.

I'm now building Cabana, a tech-enabled roll-up in pool service. We partner with wonderful pool companies, and help them scale.
